# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Trolling or sustained disruption.
- Submitting malicious, spammy, or deceptive resources.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may also ban contributors temporarily or permanently for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
